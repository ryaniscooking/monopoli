# How to Play

The in-app How to Play page is rendered from this file (spec 12.1.7). Keep to the simple format:
`## Section`, paragraphs, `- ` bullet lists and `**bold**`. Money is shown for both maps:
Dubai (AED) / Myanmar (Ks, 1,000 times larger).

## Goal

Buy places, collect rent and build houses and hotels. Players who cannot pay what they owe go **bankrupt** and leave the game. The **last player standing wins**.

If the game has a **time limit**, the current round is finished when time runs out, and the player with the highest **net worth** wins (cash + property prices + building costs; mortgaged places count at their mortgage value).

## Your turn

- Tap **Roll**. Your token moves clockwise by the total of the two dice.
- Roll **doubles** and you roll again after dealing with where you landed.
- Three doubles in a row send you straight to **Jail**.
- Pass or land on **GO** to collect your salary: AED 200 / 200,000 Ks.
- When you are done, tap **End turn**.

## Landing on a place

- **Nobody owns it:** buy it at its price, or decline. If you decline, the bank **auctions** it to everyone, you included.
- **Someone else owns it:** pay them rent. Mortgaged places charge no rent.
- **Tax:** pay the bank.
- **Chance / Community Chest:** draw a card and do what it says. Keep **Get Out of Jail Free** cards until you need them, or trade them.
- **Go To Jail:** go straight to Jail without passing GO.
- **Free Parking** and **Just Visiting:** nothing happens.

## Rent

- A place with no buildings charges its base rent, **doubled** when the owner has the whole color set and none of it is mortgaged.
- Houses and hotels raise the rent a lot. Tap any tile on the board to see its title deed.
- **Transport** (metro, trains, boats): 25 / 50 / 100 / 200 for owning 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 of them (AED; ×1,000 in Ks).
- **Utilities:** 4 times the dice total for one, 10 times for both.

## Building

- Own a **whole color set** with nothing mortgaged, then open **Manage** to build.
- Build **evenly**: a set's places may differ by at most one house.
- Four houses, then a **hotel**. The bank has 32 houses and 12 hotels; when they run out and several players want them, they are auctioned.
- Selling buildings back gives you half their cost.

## Auctions

- Everyone still in the game may bid, cash only. Each bid must beat the last one.
- The timer restarts after each bid. When it runs out, or everyone else drops out, the highest bidder pays and gets the place.
- If nobody bids, the place stays with the bank.

## Mortgages

- Mortgage a place (with no buildings in its set) to get half its price now. It earns no rent while mortgaged.
- To lift the mortgage, pay the mortgage value plus 10%.
- If you receive a mortgaged place in a trade, you pay 10% of its mortgage value at once.

## Jail

- Before rolling you may **pay the fine** (AED 50 / 50,000 Ks) or **use a Get Out of Jail Free card**. Otherwise roll for doubles.
- Doubles free you: move by that roll, but do not roll again.
- After three failed tries you must pay the fine and move.
- In Jail you can still collect rent, build, mortgage, trade and bid.

## Owing money

- If you owe more than your cash, sell buildings, mortgage places or trade until you can **Pay**.
- If you still cannot pay, you are **bankrupt**. Owing a player: everything you have goes to them. Owing the bank: your places go back to the bank and are auctioned.

## Trading

- Tap **Trade**, choose a player, then pick places, cash and Jail cards from both sides and send the offer.
- Tap a place to see its title deed. **View cards** shows every deed in the offer side by side.
- Places in a set that has buildings cannot be traded. No loans or promises: only what is in the offer changes hands.
- Offers expire after 60 seconds. You can have one open offer at a time.

## House rules

The **Official** rules follow the classic rulebook. Choose **House rules** to change: starting cash, GO salary (and double salary for landing on GO), a Free Parking pot for taxes and fines, auctions on decline, even building, unlimited houses, double rent on a full set, no rent while in Jail, managing on other players' turns, the Jail fine, auction timer and a game time limit.

## Playing online

- **Create room** and share the 6-letter code or link. Friends tap **Join**, enter the code and tap **Ready**. The host can add bots and starts the game.
- If your phone loses connection, a bot plays your seat after a short wait. Open the app again and you get your seat back.
- Online rooms can use a turn timer. Miss it three times in a row and a bot takes over your seat.

## Board controls

- **Tap** a tile to zoom in and see its deed. **Double-tap** to zoom back out.
- **Pinch** to zoom and **drag** to move around the board.
- In **Settings**: sound, animation speed, reduced motion, camera follow and color-blind letters (B, L, P, O, R, Y, G, D) on color groups.
